    How a note gets here

    Open source end to end · repository · decision records

    1. 01 · Capture

      A source with a date

      An essay, a paper, a talk. Nothing enters without its publication date and licence.

    2. 02 · Librarian

      LLM-Wiki-V2, CROW mode

      A local classifier decides where a note belongs; Claude writes it. Cost per note stays flat as the wiki grows.

    3. 03 · Format

      Open Knowledge Format v0.2

      Markdown with provenance in the frontmatter, kept in Git. Any tool can read it; nothing is locked in.

    4. 04 · Review

      Checks, then a human

      Schema, dates, links and a confidential-terms scan on every change. Only Muthu's approval marks a note reviewed.

    5. 05 · Graph

      NetworkX, with Semantica

      A deterministic graph of notes, sources and relations; Semantica adds extracted entities, kept apart and labelled.

    6. 06 · Publish

      Quartz 5 and Sigma.js 4

      Each merge cuts a release; this site and graph rebuild from it. Markdown twins and llms.txt for machines.

    Reading the map

    • A theme: one of the five folders the librarian files into
    • A note, coloured by its theme; bigger means more connections
    • Essay, initiative, playbook
    • A ring: reviewed by Muthu, with the date
    • A link in the text, or a declared relation
    • An entity Semantica extracted (off by default)

    Every note is also plain Markdown at its address plus .md; the whole wiki is in llms.txt. Text under CC BY 4.0.
